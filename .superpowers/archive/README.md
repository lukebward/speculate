# Archived plans and specifications

These documents preserve the decisions behind releases through v0.19.0.
Unchecked boxes in an old implementation plan do not identify current work.
Paths, commands, constraints, and release authorization inside these documents
refer to their original sessions.

The July and early August plans, and the September local-learning plan, describe
work shipped in earlier releases. The August 30 day-to-day utility plan combines
shipped work with an unfinished daily-workflow runner. That runner was never
implemented; its unused generator, comparison API, and associated tests were removed in
v0.20.0. The paired repeated-workflow harness is the maintained instrument.
The related August 30 specifications remain here as design and measurement
history, not a commitment to implement the abandoned runner.

Plans and their specifications moved together so their relative links still
resolve. Current instructions are in [Contributing](../../CONTRIBUTING.md) and
the [benchmark methodology](../../docs/design/local-learning-benchmark.md).
